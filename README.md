# Wings & Wires · 羽与界

> *When birds cross borders freely, who decides that humans cannot?*

An interactive data visualization juxtaposing migratory bird tracking data 
with human migration records across two geographic chapters: the Mediterranean 
and the Americas.

**Live demo:** [coming soon]  
**Status:** In development — Week 1 of 8

---

## The Argument

Bird migration data is precise, continuous, and scientifically meticulous. 
Human migration data is fragmented, politically categorized, and full of 
deliberate gaps. This project makes that asymmetry visible — not by filling 
in the missing data, but by letting the absence speak.

---

## Two Chapters

| Chapter | Bird Data | Human Data |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Mediterranean | White Stork (Movebank) | IOM Missing Migrants |
| Americas | Blackpoll Warbler / Swainson's Hawk | US Border encounters + gaps |

---

## Data Sources

**Bird migration**
- [Movebank](https://movebank.org) — GPS tracking, individual-level, timestamped

**Human migration**
- [IOM Missing Migrants Project](https://missingmigrants.iom.int) — incident-based, incomplete by nature
- [CBP Encounter Data](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/stats) — politically filtered
